Privacy Policy

This is the privacy policy for Tsukami Hero — Claw & Blade ("the app").

Last updated: 2026-11-25
Operator: Takeru Games
Contact: takerugames4@gmail.com

What is collected and why

The app never asks you to enter your real name, email address or phone number. The following information is handled through the outside services the app uses.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app uses Google AdMob to show video ads. To deliver ads and measure them, the advertising identifier (IDFA), device type, approximate location (as far as it can be told from the IP address), records of ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic information about how the app runs (such as crash logs and launch time) may be sent to Google.

  • If you choose "Ask App Not to Track" in the tracking prompt, the game plays the same. In that case you do not get ads tailored to you
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can review your choice any time from Settings → "Ad consent" in the game
  • See Google's policies and terms for how Google handles information

In-app purchases (Apple, RevenueCat)

The monthly plan and the lifetime purchase are made through Apple's App Store. The app uses RevenueCat to check whether a purchase is valid, and a record of the purchase (product, time, an anonymous user ID) is sent to RevenueCat. The app never receives payment details such as card numbers.

  • RevenueCat's privacy policy

Ranking (Supabase)

For the "Weekly Trial" ranking, the following is sent to and stored on servers run by Supabase.

  • An anonymous ID created automatically on your device (not tied to your name or to a device identifier)
  • The week you played, your score, and how many runs you made that week
  • The time a run started and ended (to spot dishonest records; deleted after 14 days)
  • The name shown on the ranking (if you chose one; up to 10 characters)
  • The title you chose, with its frame and background
  • Your hero's gear at the time of the run (kind, level, stars, number of bonus effects), your best stage and your power

The ranking shows your name, title, score and number of runs. If you have not chosen a name, an automatic name made from the anonymous ID is shown.
When someone taps a name on the ranking, they can see that player's profile (name, title, gear used in the trial, best stage, power and past ranks).
If a name or record on the ranking is a problem, it can be reported from that player's profile. A report stores the reporter's anonymous ID, the reported player's anonymous ID and name, and the reason, and the operator uses it to check the report.
If you choose "Start over" in Settings, your ranking records on the server (name, titles, scores, gear) are deleted too (this may fail while offline). Deleting the app alone does not delete the records on the server. To have them deleted, contact us at the address above.

  • Supabase's privacy policy

Game data

Your progress (save data) is stored on your device. Apart from what is sent to the ranking above, it is not sent anywhere.

Sharing with third parties

Apart from what is sent to the services above, information is not provided to third parties except where the law requires it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of information about you, and to withdraw consent. The app holds no account or contact details for you, so please include the name shown on the ranking when you contact us, and we will delete the records tied to it.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the contact above.
