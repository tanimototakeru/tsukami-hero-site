Frequently asked questions

My Premium purchase is not showing

Open Settings → Premium → "Restore purchases".

I want to cancel the monthly plan

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name at the top → Subscriptions.

I want to delete my data

In the game, Settings → "Start over" deletes the data on your device and your ranking records on the server.

I cannot start the Weekly Trial

The Weekly Trial needs a connection. Please try again where the signal is good.

I want to change my ad consent

Where consent is required (for example in Europe), Settings → "Ad consent" opens the choices again.
